# ⛰️ SATHI ADVENTURE
### ADVENTURE - "Where Every Journey Begins"

## 📋 About

At **SATHI ADVENTURE**, we're passionate about travel and adventure. Our mission is to bring the world's wonders closer to you and help you make the most of your travel experiences.

We believe every journey is an opportunity to learn, grow, and connect with people and places around the globe.

This is a simple, responsive tourism website built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**.

## ⛰️ Features

SATHI ADVENTURE is your passport to exploring exciting destinations and discovering events, tours, and hidden gems. The platform is designed to inspire visitors and help them plan their next journey.

- **Home:** A welcoming landing page with captivating travel visuals.
- **Events:** A section showcasing travel events and activities.
- **Explore:** Discover destinations and travel inspiration.
- **Tours:** Browse tour ideas and destination highlights.
- **About:** Learn about the website and its purpose.
- **Contributions:** Acknowledges contributors and inspirations.
- **Contact:** A contact form with country selection and contact details.
- **Theme toggle:** Switch between dark and light themes.
- **Responsive navigation:** Navigation designed for different screen sizes.
